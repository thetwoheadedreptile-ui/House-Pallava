# Thondaimandalam — Design Brief (v0.1)

Locked design. Lore decisions below are canonical.

## Premise

Thondaimandalam, ~500 sq km inside the **Varunavanshi Empire** — one of ten
friendly Adhika empires that rule mankind. The Adhikas: herbivore hominids,
taller and stronger than humans, negligible senescence (live 500–800 years),
1 male : 5 female sex ratio, brown hair, swan-white skin, deep blue eyes.

Hierarchy: **Emperor (Varunavanshi) → 23 provincial houses → House Vaidhya →
5 sworn houses: Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas, Ayyanars, Pallavas → Pallava's
vassals: Adigaman, Suryacholar, Parambu, Kadalon.**

You play **Mayon Pallava, 20**, eldest son of Karupi. Father **Rajan (65)**
rules as AI regent in early sprints — administrator (excellent) and politician
(weak). Ravan (63) runs production — great farmer, weak breeder. Sudalai (50)
runs marketing through the Kadalons — genius, unmarried (running gag → quest).
Young cast: Seyon (19), Vendhan (17), twins Angavai & Sangavai (17), Kotravai
(16), Kadalon (15), Avai (14), cousin Sangha (20). **Sons-only** succession and
domains; daughters are the future marriage-diplomacy system.

## Core economy (all numbers canonical)

- House Pallava is the **only** sworn house that breeds animals; the others
  have fixed industries. Rivals compete for Vaidhya's favour, never our
  contracts.
- Flagship exports: **Rajapalayam** (sleek, powerful, loyal hound) and
  **Kombai** lines to buyers from *other* empires — legal and lucrative.
- Sales split: **70% Pallava / 30% Vaidhya**.
- Tithe: **10% of all produce** paid at harvest/whelping, enforcement lax.
- Loss years: Vaidhya covers the loan; you owe him next season (Ledger entry).
- Lord Vaidhya: soft, gullible, breathtakingly handsome, looks 37 at 450.
- His **wives** enforce taxes brutally and conduct audits.

## Core tension (v0.1 loop)

Skim the tithe for margin → risk a wives' inspection → punishment + a
**Ledger** grudge that outlives every human alive. The Ledger is persistent,
house-level, inherited by heirs.

## Two clocks

Human regents age and die (~50–60 relevant years); the Adhikas barely change.
Succession is a core system: heirs inherit skills, traits, and the Ledger.
Lord Vaidhya personally outlives ~10 regents.

## Endgame directions (later sprints)

Standing/prestige restoration arc; marriage-elevation; and the unnamed
outside threat the Adhika empires warily watch — discovered first through
House Kadalon's seafaring expeditions. No combat wins restoration; violence
is structurally unavailable.
